How pickup works

Available in most urban areas. Choose "Pickup & delivery" when you book, and select a date and time window.

  • Courier arrives within your chosen time window
  • Device is logged and photographed for your record
  • You receive a confirmation the moment it's collected

How delivery works

Once repaired and quality-checked, your device is delivered back to the same address.

  • Delivery is scheduled once your ticket status reaches "Ready"
  • Live tracking updates as it's on the way
  • Sign for the device on arrival — repair notes included
